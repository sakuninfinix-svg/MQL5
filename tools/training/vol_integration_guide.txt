3. Load model at EA init:
   In PASR_PRERELEASE.mq5 or PASRKernel.mqh, add to OnInit():
   if(!g_VolPredictor.Load("PASR_vol_ridge.bin"))
      Print("WARNING: Volatility model not loaded");

4. Update persistence each bar:
   In OnTick() or pipeline DataSync stage:
   double closeBuff[];
   ArraySetAsSeries(closeBuff, true);
   CopyClose(_Symbol, PERIOD_CURRENT, 0, 16, closeBuff);
   float vol8 = ComputeRealizedVol(closeBuff, 8);
   g_VolPredictor.UpdateVolatility(vol8);

5. Effect:
   - Low vol (< 0.00005): risk multiplier → 3.0x (increase position)
   - Normal vol (~0.00027): risk multiplier → 1.0x 
   - High vol (> 0.0008): risk multiplier → ~0.3x (reduce position)
