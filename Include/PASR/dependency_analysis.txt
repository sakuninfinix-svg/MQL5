## Module Split Summary (v2.0)

### 2.Config.Types.mqh
Contains:
- All ENUMs (ENUM_PATTERN_TYPE, ENUM_CONFIG_FIELD_ID, ENUM_EVENT_ID, etc.)

### 2.Config.Manager.mqh
Contains:
